About us

Research with a practical mindset.

Axian Research is a research and development (R&D) company dedicated to transforming innovative ideas into practical, scalable solutions.

Founded in 2024 and headquartered in the United States, Axian Research operates in Hungary, combining international perspective with local engineering and research capabilities.

Our primary focus is the development of innovative, efficient, and affordable propulsion and powertrain technologies, alongside exploring software and computer solutions. We explore new approaches to engineering and technology with the goal of turning promising concepts into solutions that can be realistically implemented and scaled.

At Axian Research, we believe innovation should be simple, affordable, and practical.
